# Blog fact-check log

Every number, regulation and named claim in the 15 published posts, with where it came from. Retrieved on **September 19, 2026**. If a source changes, update the post and its `dateModified`.

**How to read the status column**
- **Verified** means I read the primary source (or a page quoting it) on the date above.
- **Arithmetic** means it is a definition or a calculation, not a claim about the world.
- **General knowledge** means a widely known winemaking fact stated without a number; no external source was cited.
- **Secondary** means the source is not the regulator or producer; re-check before relying on it for a label or contract.
